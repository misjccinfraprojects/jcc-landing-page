About The Company

Four decades of building India's infrastructure

JCC Infraprojects Private Limited was incorporated on 28th January 2014 under Part IX of the Companies Act, 1956, following the conversion of Jain Construction Company (JCC) — a partnership firm engaged in civil construction since 1986.

With over 40 years of experience, JCC is known for quality workmanship and ethical business practice, committed to best-in-class engineering solutions for timely, efficient project delivery across India's railway, road and bridge infrastructure.
